About & methodology

Objective

Cutis Lens is an educational prototype that grades acne severity from a facial photo and explains each prediction with a Grad-CAM heatmap. It is not a diagnostic tool.

Dataset

ACNE04, a public acne dataset released by Wu et al. (ICCV 2019) containing facial images labelled by severity level (levels 0–3 as described by Zhang et al., 2024). Splits are stratified, seeded and checked for duplicate-file leakage; the exact split is saved so experiments are reproducible. Labels were verified by the project author against the dataset documentation.

Model

An ImageNet-pretrained EfficientNet-B0 (transfer learning) with a new classification head, trained with class-weighted cross-entropy, AdamW, a cosine learning-rate schedule and early stopping on validation macro-F1. Training images receive mild augmentation (flip, small rotation/crop, brightness/contrast); validation and test images are only resized and normalised.

Explainable AI

Grad-CAM weights the feature maps of the last convolutional block by the average gradient of the predicted class score, keeps the positive part, and upsamples it onto the photo. Warm colours mark regions that increased the score. Limitations: it is a coarse, low-resolution attention map; it explains the model, not the skin; highlighted areas are not proof of a lesion.
